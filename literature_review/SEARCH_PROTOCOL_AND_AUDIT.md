# Search and citation audit log — 21 November 2026

## Coverage and search procedures

This project undertook **structured thematic discovery and a transparent narrative evidence review**, NOT a full PRISMA systematic review. No claim of exhaustive global search, double-screened reviewers, formal risk-of-bias scores for all studies, complete full text for every item or pre-registered search protocol.

### SciSpace: multi-question academic corpus search

Ran five broad research questions and four targeted supplementary searches (natural-language terms and variants, including years where relevant):
1. EU EBA Bangladesh apparel 2011 rules of origin, access and LDC graduation
2. LDC graduation Bangladesh vs peer countries, CGE, partial equilibrium, tariffs and supplier trade
3. revealed comparative advantage, CMSA, HHI concentration, unit values and product quality
4. CN8/HS amendments, concordances, source-year comparability, origin and missing trade observations
5. structural gravity, PPML, product-level preference effects, identification and causal methods
6. EU use of preferences, legal eligibility, RoO, productivity and trade diversification
7. peer-reviewed competing evidence and limitations of RCA, CMS and alternative metrics

9. empirical sources on 8-digit EU GSP preference use and market access

Retained a deduplicated 33-source human-curated evidence matrix. Opened/refetched 20 paper records; extracted methods, results, limitations and research-gap fields on 15 papers. Displayed the strongest **20 papers in one interactive SciSpace table** with methods, results, limitations and research gaps. Extractions are model-generated summaries that were compared against paper abstracts/publisher passage where possible; do not quote them as author text without verification.

### Wiley Scholar Gateway: publisher full-text passages

Ran five full-sentence queries in two rounds on:
- EU nonreciprocal GSP/EBA, rules-of-origin and customs utilisation
- Comparative advantage and preference erosion modeling
- Export unit values and product quality measurement
- 2011 EU rules-of-origin reform in LDC apparel, methods and outcomes
- Detailed product tariffs, preference margins and trade diversion

Results included publisher passages from **Sytsma (2021)**, **Tanaka (2021)**, **Tanaka (2022)**, Davies/Nilsson, and others. Wiley passage provenance indicates result counts, retrieved dates, journal DOI and full-text section; AI-generated summaries must be verified in original source. The retrieved passages covered publications from approximately 2005 through 2026 depending on query, not all disciplines uniformly.

Publisher-derived insights: Sytsma's 2021 paper defines customs preference use as preferential-claimed / preference-eligible import value and uses 2001–2018 Eurostat import-by-regime data. Rules-of-origin matter to take-up. The 2022 Tanaka Cambodia observed withdrawal result is a relevant analog but not Bangladesh's future effect. Some areas returned peripheral papers that were excluded from the focused matrix.

## Inclusion and exclusion rationale

Include: primary peer-reviewed trade economics/EU legal scholarship; landmark trade theories with direct construct relevance; rigorous identification of preference shocks; official Eurostat/UN/EU/TARIC legal and statistical records; recent studies relevant to Bangladesh, EBA preference erosion, CN8 and competitor countries.

Deprioritize: non-peer-reviewed student theses making claims without identifiable data, AI-generated bibliographies, unsourced blog predictions, country tariff claims without date/qualifying conditions, articles solely about unrelated tariff products, and duplicates/preprints when a final publisher version exists. Working papers included only with explicit type label if methodologically important.

## What is and is not verified

**Verified:** Identity of numerous DOI/title pairs; no indicated retractions in returned audit; key abstracts and some Wiley publisher full-text passages; existence and controlling dates of primary UN/EU legislation; documented official Eurostat CN8/partner method; internal historical and 2025 Task G reproducibility results.

**Still unverified:** Every word of 33 original full texts, all journal issue/pages metadata in APA format, future tariff schedules, each 2025 product's preference utilization, certified 2012–25 CN8 harmonization and supervisor approval. Search synthesis is an academic preparatory stage, not a formal scientific meta-analysis.
