# Novelty, research contribution and MITB supervisor brief

**Working title unchanged:** Assessing Bangladesh’s Post-LDC Graduation Market-Access Exposure in the European Union: A Product-Level Analysis of Tariff Preference Erosion and Export Competitiveness.

## Accurate novelty statement

**Do NOT claim:** "The first study of Bangladesh's LDC graduation" (Rahman & Strutt already model it), "the first detailed EU preferential tariff product-level study" (Cipollina et al. use CN8), "no studies of preference utilization in Bangladesh" (Sytsma 2021/2022), "no EU rules of origin structural work" (Curran & Nadvi), or "we calculate export losses due to tariff increases" (no behavioral model).

**Proposed defensible contribution:** A transparent, up-to-date, Bangladesh-specific **2025 CN8 EU product-level exposure and competitiveness accounting platform**, combining:
1. Bangladesh's leading 200 EU import product values and official EU extra-EU all-origin product shares;
2. China/India/Vietnam/Pakistan/Cambodia/Türkiye comparators, matched net-mass unit values, conditional competitor duties and residuals;
3. dated EU 2026 tariff candidates across multiple alternative legal paths, full line-by-line rate coverage, nonimputation and static duty-equivalent arithmetic;
4. Article 33 Section S11b 37% and 6% **different denominators**, and EBA exemption, with carefully dated roster backcasts;
5. sensitivity across product subsets, exact/complex classifications, origin groupings and missing-rate assumptions;
6. independent source checksum QA and public machine-readable, reproducible scripts;
7. interpretation updated to **2029 UN graduation** and **conditional approximately 2032 EBA exit** rather than a now-stale 2026 graduation timeline.

The originality is in the **integration, source-date vintage, Bangladesh focus, transparent product resolution, comparative benchmark and auditability**, not an unprecedented economic mechanism or identification strategy. Full novelty against all published 2026 work is a **provisional literature assessment**, not a certified uniqueness claim.

## Proposed general objective (for supervisor)

To assess how Bangladesh's product-level export basket in the EU is distributed across markets and potential tariff-preference exposure under alternative post-LDC EU market-access scenarios, relative to major competitor origins, using reproducible EU administrative trade data and legally explicit conditional measures.

## Four measurable specific objectives

1. Measure **2025** Bangladesh CN8 trade-value concentration and product-specific extra-EU import market shares for the selected 200 most valuable lines.
2. Compare Bangladesh with six competing origins using product-level EU import values, market shares, valid EUR/kg ratios and source-dated conditional tariff advantages.
3. Calculate **static conditional duty equivalents** and qualification coverage under reference-date 2026 MFN, Standard GSP/GSP+ analogues and future-hypothetical legal paths, explicitly excluding missing rates instead of inventing them.
4. Screen the **historical numeric** Article 33 S11b threshold across 2023–2025 under an explicit **2027 beneficiary-roster backcast**, and report limitations, sensitivity and EBA safeguard exception.

Alternative fifth objective if approved: assess robustness to classification, sector composition and partner country sample without inferring causality.

## Candidate tightly aligned research questions

- RQ1: How concentrated is Bangladesh's top-200 2025 EU-origin product basket, and which product lines have the greatest source-defined EU extra-EU market shares?
- RQ2: Which products show the highest *conditional* 2026 tariff advantage or disadvantage relative to the six comparator origins, and how much trade is excluded for missing or legally conditional rate information?
- RQ3: How do fixed-2025-value conditional duty equivalents differ across tariff regime benchmarks, top-n and classification groupings?
- RQ4: What do official HS2 Chapters 61–63 annual section import data imply for a 2027-roster Article 33 numerical backcast, and why is this different from actual safeguard applicability?
- RQ5 (optional): How sensitive are exploratory product-level tariff/competitiveness associations to HS2 adjustment and quantity/missingness filters?

## Suggested theoretical framework

**Primary lens:** tariff preference and comparative market-access incentives, with destination-market share and relative supplier tariffs measured at the same product resolution. **Secondary:** industrial trade competitiveness and firm heterogeneity, GVC/sourcing rules as *interpretive mechanisms* supported by scholarly sources, not direct measured outcomes.

Conceptual structure:

Trade institutions (UN LDC designation; EU EBA / Standard GSP / GSP+ / MFN; RoO and actual use)
→ source-dated *conditional tariff margin and relative supplier cost advantage*
→ possible response channels (demand, sourcing, buyer decisions, pass-through; **not observed causally**)
→ observable EU imports, product market shares and EUR/kg ratios.

Sector, product mix, existing supply chains and non-tariff barriers confound market outcomes; the 2025 data measure the outcome patterns and hypothetical margins, not arrows' causal magnitudes.
