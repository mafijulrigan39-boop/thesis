## Improvements to the prior Section 3.1
1. More explicit theory selection criterion: target estimand + grain + period + identification capacity.
2. Clearly label “preferential market access” as a legal/economic trade-cost mechanism, **not** a formally estimated stand-alone gravity model.
3. Distinguish legal **statutory** candidate, origin-qualified **applicable** tariff, and customs **used** preference; missing EU claim data acknowledged.
4. Place UN graduation timing and separate EBA legal transition in **institutional constraints**, not predictions of tariffs.
5. Distinguish observed EU market share from Balassa RCA; EUR/kg from quality or productivity; top-200 HHI from national concentration.
6. Explain why GVC/heterogeneous firm theory contextual; OLI and institutional-quality theory not primary.
7. Treat 2025 trade vs 2026 tariff snapshot as two differently dated sources. Do not assert causality from their association.
8. Do not reveal own numerical results or falsely confirm post-EBA effects inside 3.1.
9. The variable roles, actual equations and diagram remain appropriately allocated to forthcoming 3.2 and 3.3.
