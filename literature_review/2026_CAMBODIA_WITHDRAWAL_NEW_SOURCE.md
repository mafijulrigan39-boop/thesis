# Additional 2026 empirical source — Cambodia preference withdrawal

**Discovery date:** 21 November 2026

Snameas, P., Tanaka, K., & Tsubota, K. (2026). *Does the EU's withdrawal of tariff preferences induce trade redirection? Evidence from Cambodia* (Discussion Paper No. 1018). Institute of Developing Economies, Japan External Trade Organization.
https://www.ide.go.jp/English/Publish/Reports/Dp/1018.html

**Source class:** Published institutional working paper / discussion paper, **not shown to be peer reviewed**, and **not** a completed systematic-literature review inclusion.

**Geography/time:** Cambodian export observations, 2017–2024; EU partial EBA withdrawal in August 2020; examined products treated versus untreated by the policy.

**Design:** Difference-in-differences analysis of Cambodian exports to non-EU destinations, where treated/untreated products arise from the selective 2020 withdrawal.

**Primary source abstract:** No detected increase of affected product exports to non-EU destinations; reported exports of affected footwear products fell about 50%. Authors posit GVC buyer relationships as a possible mechanism, not a directly identified causal mediation channel.

**Boundaries:** Cambodia, specific affected products, 2020 shock (including possible coincident conditions), non-EU diversion outcome; cannot assume numerically identical treatment effect for future Bangladesh EU preference loss. The paper is extremely recent and has not undergone full independent peer review.

**Use in thesis:** Section 2.2.3 and critical comparison matrix, separated from peer-reviewed Tanaka (2022). Could motivate discussion of constraints on trade redirection, not prove Bangladesh post-EBA export losses.

**Verification source:** Official IDE-JETRO publisher page at link above. This is a new source supplement to the previously screened 33-paper literature corpus; the original reading register remains a dated record rather than silently renumbered.
