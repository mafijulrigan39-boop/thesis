# Verified and screened academic reading list

Contains 33 distinct scholarly studies and working papers. Entries with a DOI have links, but full journal issue/pages/author strings should be reconstructed from primary publisher metadata for the final APA bibliography, rather than fabricated. Read status: SciSpace record for some, extracted methods/results for 15, Wiley full-text passages for a subset, and Consensus fetched abstracts for several. Not all complete full texts read.

- **01 | Balassa 1965** — Trade theory / RCA. Journal index and comparative advantage. [Publisher/DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9957.1965.tb00050.x) **Verification:** verified. **For thesis:** Revealed specialization is not proven productivity; requires defined global benchmark.
- **02 | Krugman 1980** — Trade theory / differentiation. Scale economies and intra-industry trade theory. DOI not verified: consult original. **Verification:** verified title/authors. **For thesis:** Contextual theory; no dataset identifies its mechanism directly.
- **03 | Melitz 2003** — Trade theory / firms. Heterogeneous firms select into export markets. [Publisher/DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/1468-0262.00467) **Verification:** verified. **For thesis:** Not testable with only country-product import values.
- **04 | Gereffi Humphrey Sturgeon 2005** — GVC theory. Global value chain governance and upgrading. [Publisher/DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/09692290500049805) **Verification:** verified. **For thesis:** Auxiliary explanatory context; no firm/buyer linkages measured.
- **05 | Anderson van Wincoop 2003** — Trade theory / gravity. Structural gravity and multilateral resistance. [Publisher/DOI](https://doi.org/10.1257/000282803321455214) **Verification:** verified. **For thesis:** Requires appropriately varying bilateral and destination observations.
- **06 | Santos Silva Tenreyro 2006** — Trade econometrics. PPML and heteroscedastic trade flow estimation. [Publisher/DOI](https://doi.org/10.1162/rest.88.4.641) **Verification:** verified. **For thesis:** Do not claim current cross section is a causal PPML trade-policy model.
- **07 | Arkolakis Costinot Rodríguez-Clare 2012** — Trade welfare theory. Calibration-based welfare changes under trade models. [Publisher/DOI](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.102.1.94) **Verification:** verified. **For thesis:** No welfare/elasticity estimates in this thesis.
- **08 | Brenton 2003** — EU EBA policy research. Preferential access varies by product, MFN baseline and RoO. [Publisher/DOI](https://doi.org/10.1596/1813-9450-3018) **Verification:** verified. **For thesis:** Original World Bank working-paper DOI; historical year, not current tariff law.
- **09 | Sytsma 2021** — EU GSP utilisation. Triple-difference EU 2011 RoO reform; utilisation responds to compliance rules. [Publisher/DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/coep.12529) **Verification:** verified; Wiley full-text passages. **For thesis:** Bangladesh customs tariff eligibility is not necessarily actual preference use.
- **10 | Sytsma 2022** — Bangladesh apparel firms. Transaction-level effects of 2011 EU sourcing-rule change on exporters. [Publisher/DOI](https://doi.org/10.1257/pol.20200257) **Verification:** verified. **For thesis:** Cannot infer firm outcomes from aggregated EU product imports.
- **11 | Nilsson 2011** — EU GSP utilisation. Small trade flows show systematically lower preference-use rates. [Publisher/DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1813-6982.2011.01309.x) **Verification:** verified. **For thesis:** Aggregate use is not line-level use.
- **12 | Davies Nilsson 2019** — Preferential agreements. EU-versus-US LDC preferential schemes and rates of utilisation. [Publisher/DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/dpr.12434) **Verification:** verified. **For thesis:** Different trade regimes cannot be treated as equivalent.
- **13 | Tanaka 2021** — Cambodia RoO reform. Difference-in-differences on EU apparel sourcing-rule reform. [Publisher/DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/twec.13108) **Verification:** verified; Wiley full text. **For thesis:** Cambodia 2011 causal estimates not Bangladesh post-LDC causal effects.
- **14 | Tanaka 2022** — Cambodia EBA withdrawal. Real-world partial EU preference withdrawal event for Cambodia. ## Critical corrections
- Bekkers and Cariola (2025) is the **final journal year** (volume 34, issue 3, pages 397–427), despite DOI and online-first publication dating 2024.
- French's published Journal of International Economics DOI is **10.1016/j.jinteco.2017.02.002**; SSRN 10.2139/ssrn.2525827 refers to earlier working paper.
- Hummels and Klenow's AER DOI is **10.1257/0002828054201396**. A different previously guessed number was invalid.
- Hallak 2006 is **10.1016/j.jinteco.2005.04.001**; another guessed identifier linked to an unrelated work.
- Krugman 1980 journal article's actual title is *Scale Economies, Product Differentiation, and the Pattern of Trade*. It does not need a guessed DOI.
- Brenton 2003 DOI above identifies a World Bank working-paper version; do not mistake it for a journal-publisher DOI.
- Cirera et al. 2011 remains a **working-paper bibliographic entry** until the exact manifestation is verified.
- 2026 EU legislation is authored by European Parliament and Council, not simply DG TRADE.
