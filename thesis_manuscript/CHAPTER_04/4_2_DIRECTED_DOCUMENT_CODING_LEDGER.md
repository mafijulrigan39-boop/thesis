# Section 4.2 — Directed legal-document thematic coding ledger

**Created:** 22 November 2026
**Design:** Researcher-produced, **deductive document content analysis** for eligibility and scenario-variable definition, not an independent participant-based qualitative research study. **Provisional author coding; not double-coded or supervisor-validated.**

## Corpus

- **EU-GSP-2026**: European Parliament and Council, Regulation (EU) 2026/1395, Official Journal, 22 June 2026 (applies from 1 Jan 2027). Primary text: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/1395/oj/eng
- **UN-LDC-2026**: United Nations General Assembly, Resolution A/RES/81/8 (2026), official status also corroborated by UN LDC Portal: https://www.un.org/ldcportal/en/content/bangladesh-graduation-status . Primary resolution: https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4132162/files/A_RES_81_8-EN.pdf
- **EU-GSP-REPORT-2026**: European Commission & High Representative, Joint Report on GSP 2023–2025, CELEX:52026JC0016: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX:52026JC0016

**Source status:** Normative legal provisions (EU Regulation) have primacy for eligibility and procedures; the UN resolution controls graduation timetable; the Commission report is administrative/interpretive context and does not amend legislation. Exact archived TARIC product rates belong to the tariff matrix, not to this corpus.

## Predefined codes

- **T1:** Scheme architecture and beneficiary eligibility
- **T2:** LDC graduation and EBA transition
- **T3:** Tariff-product treatment, origin and actual use
- **T4:** Safeguard denominator and relevant section threshold
- **T5:** Safeguard exceptions and administrative activation
- **T6:** Provenance, dated rosters and statistical authority

## Audit actions and reproducibility

1. Retain each original primary URL plus its article/annex locator; source role controls legal interpretation.
2. Code from original legal/procedural text; preserve the meaningful rule, its exception and effective/eligibility qualification.
3. Do not replace statutory origin requirements with assumed actual utilization, or convert unknown rates to zero.
4. Cross-check T4–T5 against independent original EU Article 33 and Annex IV provisions; maintain 37% vs 6% denominators.
5. Reopen this ledger if the EU issues amendments, annex updates, delegated acts or implementing acts, and record retrieval/legal-effective dates.
6. Codebook and interpretations were produced by the researcher-assisted drafting process; **not** independently double-coded or validated by an external legal reviewer.
7. Prior to thesis submission, supervisor and if appropriate qualified customs/legal review should confirm the operative instruments and coding decisions.

**Method reference:** Hsieh and Shannon (2005), https://doi.org/10.1177/1049732305276687; Bowen (2009), https://doi.org/10.3316/QRJ0902027. This is **directed content analysis**, not a full inductive Braun–Clarke reflexive thematic analysis.
