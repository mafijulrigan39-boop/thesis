## Do not silently resolve these real source ambiguities

- The **main outline** specifies chapters 1–7 plus “10. References” and “11. In-text Citation Rules”. “10” and “11” are guideline section labels, **not independently confirmed thesis chapter numbers**.
- Section **1.7**, PDF page 10, provides a **five-chapter example** inconsistent with the seven-chapter main outline. Work from the seven-chapter outline unless the supervisor explicitly instructs otherwise.
- The guideline includes detailed directions for Chapters **1–4** through 4.5; Chapters **5–7 are only specified by the overview**, not given separate detailed pages.
- APA citation style is explicitly mandated, but **APA edition, page margins, font, page limits, abstract format, title pages, table-of-contents style and plagiarism thresholds are not specified** in these files. APA 7 is a sensible provisional convention **subject to approval**, not presented as the guideline's explicit edition.
- “Regression/SEM/surveys/interviews” are **examples of methods**, not required analysis regardless of research design; use only performed and defensible methods.
- Final “7 Conclusion” is explicit; the guideline's conclusion format is summary, contributions, final statement, not a license to invent findings.
- We must preserve all data certifications and limitations from \`task_g\`; the source document's style instructions cannot turn scenarios into verified causal or legal forecasts.
