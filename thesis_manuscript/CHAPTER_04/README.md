# Chapter 4 — Research Methods

**Working thesis:** Assessing Bangladesh’s Post-LDC Graduation Market-Access Exposure in the European Union: A Product-Level Analysis of Tariff Preference Erosion and Export Competitiveness.

**Design decision (22 November 2026):** **Quantitative, retrospective, observational secondary-data analysis with supplementary directed legal-document content analysis**, comprising (i) descriptive 2025 CN8 product cross-section, (ii) six-origin competitor comparisons, (iii) source-date 2026 tariff-candidate qualification, (iv) fixed-value conditional scenario accounting, (v) 2023–2025 HS61–63 Article 33 legal screen, and (vi) post-hoc exploratory statistical checks.

**Non-negotiable guardrails:** Do not report source-dated 2026 rates as 2025 collected duties or certain 2032 tariffs. No causal EBA effect is identified. Article 33 2027-roster 2023–2025 ratios are illustrative and EBA remains exempt. Current numerical results are independently audited but not supervisor-approved; annual historic CN8 concordance is not source-certified. No interviews, surveys, SEM, experimental assignment or causal regression were performed. Directed legal-document coding is ancillary and does not estimate exporter behaviours.
