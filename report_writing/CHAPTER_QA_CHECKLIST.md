# Mandatory thesis writing QA checklist — sourced from 18-page guideline

Status for a chapter can be marked COMPLETE only when all applicable checks are met; chapter drafts are not approvals.

## Chapter 1 — Introduction
- [ ] Global → international policy → Bangladesh → specific EU-product issue narrative flows logically
- [ ] Definitions, national statistics, dated laws and factual claims supported by appropriate sources
- [ ] Section 1.2 distinguishes problem from theme, names genuine prior-work gap, gives documentary evidence
- [ ] Section 1.2 **does not reveal own results**, assert 'no literature', or preempt policy solutions
- [ ] General and specific objectives are measurable, feasible and linked to source data (not recommendations)
- [ ] Each research question/hypothesis maps to an objective and verifiable construct
- [ ] Significance is grounded and nonpromotional (academic, policy, managerial/economic, methodological if real)
- [ ] Scope states EU reporter/flow, code, 2025 purposive cohort, six countries, 2026 tariff date and limitations
- [ ] Section 1.7 briefly previews **seven** primary chapters without new findings
- [ ] No detailed methods, overlong literature summaries, excess unsupported general statistics

## Chapter 2 — Literature Review
- [ ] Sections 2.1–2.3 organized **by themes and comparisons**, not one study per paragraph
- [ ] Peer-reviewed theory and empirical research sources verifiable (DOI/author/journal/year)
- [ ] Both international and Bangladesh-/EU-relevant findings considered
- [ ] Literature methods, contexts, variables and contradictory findings compared
- [ ] Research gap is specific, evidence-supported and matches actually executed study
- [ ] No plagiarism, excessive copied passages, unsupported 'first-ever' statements
- [ ] APA in-text citations and corresponding reference entries correct

## Chapter 3 — Theoretical Framework
- [ ] Chosen theory justified for this trade preference/product competitiveness project
- [ ] Only relevant theorists and their major concepts introduced, supported academically
- [ ] All variable definitions and operational links align with Task F/Task G tables
- [ ] Measured correlation distinguished from theoretical mechanism and **not called causal evidence**
- [ ] One clear cited conceptual diagram illustrates proposed analytical links
- [ ] No unneeded OLI/FDI material, decorative arrows or unsupported variables

## Chapter 4 — Research Methods
- [ ] Real design stated: observational quantitative secondary data, 2025 cross-sectional + dated scenarios
- [ ] No invented interviews, surveys, case studies, SEM, Cronbach alpha, SPSS or other unused methods
- [ ] 4.2 appropriately marked N/A or treated per supervisor instruction
- [ ] Sample population and **purposive top-200 2025 CN8 selection** clearly justified
- [ ] Official EU reporter/flow, Eurostat Comext DS-045409, tariff and legal sources precisely dated
- [ ] Names competitor origins and explains 1200 potential pairs/valid units and rate status
- [ ] Code meaning and historical harmonization limits stated; MISSING not set to zero
- [ ] Equations, eligibility filters, p-values/multiple testing, classification exclusions reproducible
- [ ] Python 3.12, source checksums and successful GitHub Actions runs precisely identified
- [ ] **No results or discussions inside methodology**

## Chapter 5 — Results (source overview only)
- [ ] Descriptive statistics and test results reported neutrally without interpretations
- [ ] Exactly reproduced data from executed results (no unsourced computations or invented p-values)
- [ ] 2025 value vs 2026 source rate date and all numerators/denominators unmistakable
- [ ] Scenario tariff costs explicitly labelled STATIC, CONDITIONAL, NOT EXPORT LOSS
- [ ] H2 pooled p and HS2-adjusted null both shown, with transparent post-hoc qualifier
- [ ] 2023–25 Article33 backward roster and EBA exemption carefully labelled
- [ ] All data tables/figures have units, period, source, sample size and missingness notes
- [ ] **No literature interpretation or policy prescriptions in Results**

## Chapter 6 — Discussion (source overview only)
- [ ] Each research question and hypothesis revisited in the context of literature
- [ ] Explains why H2 sector sensitivity changes interpretation, with no causal claims
- [ ] 6.2.1 theoretical contribution precisely bounded by observable design
- [ ] 6.2.2 exporter and policy implications are conditional and measured
- [ ] 6.3 thoroughly covers missing rate/classification/quantity/source status and HHI residual
- [ ] Legal Article33 section-S11b numerator/beneficiary denominator, 37% screen and 6% exception distinguished
- [ ] Future research directions feasible, including true historical CN8 harmonization
- [ ] No unverified forecasts, assumptions promoted to actual law or conclusions

## Chapter 7 — Conclusion (source overview only)
- [ ] Key findings summarized accurately, contributions proportionate, final statement clear
- [ ] Avoids introduction of new empirical numbers, sources, methods or causal effects
- [ ] Does not repeat Discussion at length

## References and format
- [ ] Every specific factual/statistical and literature claim supported
- [ ] APA author-year one/two/three-plus and quotations with true page numbers
- [ ] All references verified against journal/publisher/official legal source, no fabricated DOIs
- [ ] Bekkers–Cariola journal issue year and 2026 EU regulation legal authorship corrected
- [ ] Figure and table captions, sequencing and cross-references consistent
- [ ] APA edition, margins, font, pagination, word count and departmental front matter confirmed (NOT stated in supplied guideline)
- [ ] Seven vs five-chapter example and '10/11' guideline numbers resolved by supervisor for final TOC

- [ ] Actual GitHub Task G validated tables and hashes are current, errors resolved
- [ ] Historical 2012–2025 CN8 claims never exceed evidence certification
