# Official legal, EU customs and statistical sources — versioned policy register

As-of date: **21 November 2026**, subject to legal revision. This register separates *official legal claims* from academic theory, analytical backcasts and current scenario assumptions. Use legal sources when citing laws, not literature summaries of the laws.

## L1. UN graduation date — controlling primary record

- United Nations General Assembly (2026). **Resolution A/RES/81/8**, adopted **29 October 2026**, operative paragraph 3: Bangladesh's preparatory period for graduation from LDC category is extended by an additional three years, **until 24 November 2029**.
- Official text: https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4132162/files/A_RES_81_8-EN.pdf
- Related UN LDC portal status: https://www.un.org/ldcportal/en/content/bangladesh-graduation-status
- **Editorial control:** Earlier original EU publications and many 2024–2026 studies describe Bangladesh as graduating in 2026, an earlier schedule now superseded. Do not inherit those old dates as current findings.

## L2. EU GSP regulation and conditional end of EBA

- European Parliament & Council of the European Union. **Regulation (EU) 2026/1395 of 17 June 2026 on applying a generalised scheme of tariff preferences and repealing Regulation (EU) No 978/2012**. Published in Official Journal 22 June 2026; framework applies from **1 January 2027** through 2036.
- Primary EUR-Lex: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/1395/oj/eng
- EU DG TAXUD GSP rules and origin/cumulation implementation: https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/customs/rules-origin-goods/preferential-rules-origin/generalised-system-preferences-gsp_en
- Commission explanatory Q&A: https://policy.trade.ec.europa.eu/development-and-sustainability/generalised-scheme-preferences/questions-answers-new-eu-generalised-scheme-preferences_en

**Three-year EBA status exit transition:** This EU regulation grants a transition of three years from when the UN-LDC condition is no longer satisfied to eventual delegated removal from EBA list. Combining the **new** 24 Nov 2029 UN graduation with the three-year EU mechanism implies a **conditional EBA transition horizon around November 2032**. This is not an approved specific duty-effective date, not a Commission delegated act, and not evidence of which regime would replace EBA. Some EU 2026 Q&A still says "graduation in 2026 / EBA through at least end 2029"; those illustrations predate the UN's October 2026 extension. Preserve both source dates and explicitly reconcile this apparent contradiction.

**Potential successor paths:** Standard GSP (sensitive/non-sensitive products, product graduation, remaining eligible lines); GSP+ (eligibility, conventions and Commission admission); separate FTA if agreed and implemented; or MFN. Actual outcome depends on applicable future legal status, eligibility, RoO, country classification, conditionality and calendar. The research's 2026 tariff rates are dated *benchmarks* and not 2032 payable rates.

## L3. Article 33 textile special safeguard

Official legal text and Annex III/IV: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/1395/oj/eng

Section S-11b textile/apparel calculation under Article 33 must use **all applicable GSP beneficiary countries** as the comparison group, **not Bangladesh's share of worldwide or all EU imports**, for the **37% threshold in Annex IV**.

Article 33's subsection 2 also states that the safeguard provision **does not apply to EBA beneficiary countries**. Additionally countries with no more than **6% of total Union imports** of the relevant products are outside the trigger. This 6% denominator is **all Union imports for same products**, not the GSP-beneficiary denominator.

The mechanism requires Commission **implementing act**, publication, legal effective date and correct annual beneficiary origin roster. Numerical ratios alone do not impose duties or establish customs liability.

Archived Task F numerical screen using **2027 GSP beneficiary roster backcast** to 2025 data (i.e. not the operative 2025 roster):
- Bangladesh S11b EU import value: EUR 19,865,351,190.
- GSP beneficiary imports under 2027-roster backcast: EUR 40,782,984,357.
- Bangladesh share of that backcast GSP denominator: **48.7099%**, numerically above 37%.
- All published extra-EU S11b import value: EUR 108,130,114,257; BD share **18.3717%**, above 6%, but this is a separate conditional test.
- Actual application while Bangladesh is EBA: **NO**, due to the EBA exception; 2023/2024 backcast sensitivity and origin residuals in Task G must remain labeled.

Do not present the historical screen as an actual safeguard implementing decision in 2025, as legal application in 2029, or as a forecast for 2032. Future GSP roster, import composition, regime eligibility and Commission acts can change.

## L4. EU official preference utilisation benchmarks

- European Commission / EEAS 2026 Joint Report on 2023–2025 EU GSP implementation (with 2024 numerical customs results): https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX:52026JC0016
- Commission country overview: https://policy.trade.ec.europa.eu/eu-trade-relationships-country-and-region/countries-and-regions/bangladesh_en
- EU GSP Hub country information: https://gsphub.eu/country-info/Bangladesh

The Commission reports Bangladesh used about **EUR 19bn of EBA preferences in 2024** with around **96% utilization**, reflecting *actual preference claims on eligible imports*, not every transaction or every 2025 product. The 2026 GSP Hub materials also refer to 2025 around 96% (a 2026 event booklet gives **96.3% on EUR 21.1bn eligible**); use those only with year, denominator definition, issuing organization and dataset provenance distinguished from Eurostat import values. The link for the event booklet is https://gsphub.eu/sites/default/files/2026-11/Event_Booklet_Digital_Final29.10.2026.pdf ; this is not a substitute for actual product-level claimed preference records.

External **UNCTAD preference utilization database** describes tariff-line data from Quad markets through 2020: https://unctad.org/topic/trade-agreements/trade-preferences-utilization . Earlier data can support methods, not factual 2025 utilization without an updated source.

**Recommended upgrade:** obtain EU GSP Hub/Commission raw preferential entry values by 2025 CN8 and country and calculate true line-level utilization; else explicitly run only 90/95/96/100% assumed-use sensitivities and label them as hypotheses, not customs observations.

## L5. Eurostat Comext statistical authority

- International trade in goods database (Comext): https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/international-trade-in-goods/database
- API documentation for DS- datasets: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/user-guides/data-browser/api-data-access/api-getting-started/comext-database
- Detailed Eurostat ITGS metadata: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/metadata/EN/ext_go_detail_sims.htm

Official Eurostat methodological statements:
- Extra-EU import **partner country is country of origin**; this differs from intra-EU EU-system imports' consignment partner definition.
- Import values can differ from Bangladesh-reported exports owing to CIF vs FOB, different special/general trade systems, timing, confidentiality, product or partner coding and later revisions.
- Product lines are CN8, whose classification can change **every year**; Eurostat publishes official successive conversion tables.
- Some source years have confidentiality or missingness. A printed zero and a missing/no-reported-data code are not interchangeable.
- The 2012 classification revision itself involved substantial code creation/deletion. Historical CN8 lines require versioned concordance and code family's composition checks.

Study's frozen source: Eurostat DS-045409, 2025 CN8 top200 cohort, six origin comparators, all-origin denominators, 2026-11-01 TARIC source date. Avoid calling 2012–2025 rows a harmonized product panel.
