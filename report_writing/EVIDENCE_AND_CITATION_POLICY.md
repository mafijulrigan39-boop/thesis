# Evidence, scholarly integrity and claim-strength policy for this MITB thesis

## Document hierarchy
- Department/supervisor written amendment > supplied 18-page *Thesis Guidelines* > approved research plan > reproducible dataset + actual executed analysis > provisional editorial house style.
- Research evidence from \`task_g/verified_results/\`, \`task_f_empirical/full/\`, \`task_f_empirical/article33/\` and the official source provenance takes precedence over earlier preliminary draft claims.

## Three distinct evidence categories
1. **Observed/statistical:** Eurostat 2025 EU imports/market shares; quantity and unit value when valid; top-200 purposive selection; verified descriptive concentration; correlations/conditional exploratory test results.
2. **Derived conditional scenario:** actual observed 2025 value * dated 2026 candidate legal duty-rate difference; EBA-loss/Standard-GSP/Article33 hypotheticals. Never claim as forecast impact or observed tariff payable in 2029/2032.
3. **Unverified/contingent:** historical CN8 product-to-product trend certification, actual 2027+ trade scheme eligibility or preference utilisation, institutional implementation, exporter behavioral elasticities, counterfactual causal export loss. No assertions of these as established results.

## Explicit findings requiring especially careful prose
- Selected 2025 CN8 top-200 Bangladesh EU import value **EUR 20,789,281,101**.
- Of 200 lines, 196 have numeric source-dated MFN candidates, covering 99.369% of the value.
- Source-date 2026 MFN conditional static equivalent is **EUR 2,443,135,906.98** on qualified lines, NOT a lost export value.
- The 2023/2024/2025 Article33 S11b historical numerical screens use the **2027** GSP beneficiary roster as a backcast; EBA status currently exempts Bangladesh.
- H1 is an ex-post exploratory 2026 tariff schedule rate gap, not effect of tariff on competitiveness.
- H2 pooled rank correlation was positive weakly, but sector-adjusted sensitivity is near zero (rho about -0.0096; p about 0.843): **do not claim robust tariff-competitiveness association**.
- H3 partner-specific EUR/kg sign tests compare customs unit values, not product quality or producer profits.

## Bibliographic controls
- Carry forward verified 14-item Task G bibliography audit; at minimum fix Bekkers & Cariola final issue **2025**, and credit European Parliament + Council (not Commission alone) for Regulation (EU) 2026/1395.
- APA author-year in-text for literature and official sources; quote only against full authoritative sources and actual location/page, not snippets or second-hand references. No invented page numbers, DOIs, source years or precise rates.
- Cite Eurostat database + collection date/query/vintage and TARIC/source-date measures, not just generic source homepages.
- Mark interpretation as interpretation (Discussion); do not convert limitations or scenario caveats into facts.

## Methods and reproducibility controls
- Report sample selection as purposive; no invented representativeness.
- In Methods, cite software/scripts and inputs that were actually executed, 11 SHA-256 source checks, and deterministic inclusion/filter logic; do not assert regressions or interviews were done because the example mentions them.
- In Results, present descriptive and inferential results only, without policy explanation or literature comparison; reserve for Discussion.
- Separate *no reported observation* from mathematical zero. Report coverage when outcome denominators differ.
- Any new numerical claim must be reproducible from an archived script, named source file and dated version, with reviewed output.
- If new official sources change legal or numerical treatment, update report version, original source, executable QA, result tables, and bibliography coherently.

## Original guideline's non-mandates
It gives **no exact** chapter word counts, citation count, page size, spacing, margin/font, APA edition, compulsory regression, or ethical approval requirements for this specific secondary dataset. Supervisor must settle these before final typesetting.

## Practical drafting sequence
1. Lock current table of contents and thesis-specific variable dictionary.
2. Build verified literature/citation matrix for Chapter 2 and theoretical lens for Chapter 3.
3. Draft Chapter 4 accurately from source and successful QA scripts; cite receipts.
4. Draft factual Chapter 5 from checked output tables without interpretation.
5. Draft Chapters 1 and 3/2 in a consistent argument (Introduction no own results).
6. Draft Chapter 6 and 7 only after results audited; include all null findings and caveats.
7. Run chapter-by-chapter QA, cross-reference and APA audit; obtain actual supervisor feedback/signature before declaring final.

The ordering above is a suggested writing workflow, **not** a mandated chapter order or proof of supervisor approval.
